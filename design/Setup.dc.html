Installation

Your MAG box, set up in four steps.

You do not need to open the box or install anything. We register your line against the box's MAC address and send you one portal URL to type in. Most customers are watching within minutes.

A stable connection

Around 15 Mbps is comfortable for HD and 25 Mbps for 4K. Wired beats wi-fi if the box is far from the router.

The box's MAC address

Printed on the sticker underneath, and shown on screen under Settings. It starts 00:1A:79 on a MAG.

About five minutes

Four short steps, one reboot. Nothing to download, no app to sideload, no settings to undo later.

1 Step 1

Find your MAC address

On the box, open Settings, then System Settings, then About. The MAC is the line beginning 00:1A:79. It is also printed on the sticker on the underside of the box.

4 Step 4

Enter it, reboot, watch

On the box: Settings, then Servers, then Portals. Paste the URL into Portal 1 URL, save, and restart the box. The full grid loads on the next boot.

If something is not right

The portal will not load

Check the URL for a missing slash at the end, then reboot the box. If it still fails, send us the MAC again — the line may be registered to a different one.

Picture freezes on one channel

Try the same channel on its backup number first. If every channel stutters, it is usually the connection — test the box on a cable before anything else.

Catch-up is missing on a channel

Not every channel carries the full fourteen days. The EPG shows which ones do — the ones with a rewind arrow beside the programme name.

You changed the box

Send us the new MAC address and we move the line across. There is no charge for a swap on an active subscription.

Stuck on a step? We will do it with you.

Message us with the MAC address and where you got to. Support answers around the clock, in plain language, without asking you to reset the box.
